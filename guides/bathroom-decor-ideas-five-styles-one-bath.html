Bathroom Decor Ideas: 5 Styles in One Small Bath

Same tile, tub, white vanity and window. Only the mirror, light, towels, mat and a few accents change. Compare five styles, then pick the swaps that fit.

By Nest & Paws · Checked Nov 19, 2026 · How I pick

A style name is hard to picture in a small bathroom. This guide shows one bath with the same subway tile, tub, white shaker vanity and window in five decor styles, so the mirror, vanity light, towels, bath mat and a few accents are the only things that change.

The images are AI-generated concept pictures, not photos of a real bathroom, and the decor shown is a style idea rather than a specific product. Most of these swaps hang, plug in or sit on a shelf, so you can try a look without a remodel.

Five styles, one small bath

Each look changes the mirror, vanity light, towels, mat and one or two accents. The tile, tub, vanity, sink and window stay the same.

Before you pick a style

Start with what stays

Tile, tub and vanity are the hardest things to change, so choose the style that works with them. Here a white vanity and white tile suit all five, and the style comes from the pieces you can swap.

Swap in this order

Renter guides from several home sites list the same easy swaps: a new mirror, vanity lighting, towels, a bath mat, a shower curtain, and a plant or a few accessories. Start with the mirror and the lighting, since they sit at eye level, then add fabrics.

Use retro in small doses

Designers quoted by LivingEtc suggest keeping bold retro pieces to a few, and starting small is a low-commitment way to try the look. In the Retro image the checkerboard curtain is the big move, so the mat and mirror stay simple.

Check the light before you buy

Some renter-friendly fixtures plug in or need no hardwiring. A fixture wired into the wall is different, so ask your landlord, or an electrician if you own the home, before you swap one. The Modern, Farmhouse, Japandi and Boho looks all lean on the light, so look at how each fixture mounts.

Keep one hero per area

Each look works because one piece leads: the checkerboard curtain, the black light bar, the sconces, the oak mirror or the rattan mirror. Pick one lead piece for the mirror wall and one for the floor, then keep the rest quiet.

Watch the downsides

A busy curtain can look loud in a small bath. Woven and wood pieces look best kept away from direct splashes, so check the maker's care notes. A plant needs a bright enough spot, so check your window light first.

If you do one thing

If you do one thing, change the mirror and the light. They set the style on the wall you see first, and you can add towels, a mat and a curtain later to match.

What I left out

  • Bathroom products, since the images show style ideas, not items for sale here.
