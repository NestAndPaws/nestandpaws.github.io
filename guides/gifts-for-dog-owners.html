Pick 1

KONG Classic Dog Toy, Large

Under $25

KONG Classic is a long-running stuffable chew toy. The hollow center holds treats or paste, which gives a dog something to work on. Both Amazon and Chewy list it at 4.6 stars.

Watch out: Sizes vary a lot. Match the size to your dog's weight on the listing before you buy.

Pick 2

Chuckit! Ultra Ball, Medium, 2-Pack

Under $25

A simple fetch gift with strong ratings at both retailers. The 2-pack means one is always ready when the other is lost in the yard.

Watch out: Medium is 2.5 inches. Check the ball size against your dog's mouth, and small dogs may need the small size.

Pick 3

FURminator Undercoat deShedding Tool, Large Dog, Long Hair

Under $50

FURminator is a known name for shedding dogs, and the tool is sold in sizes by dog size and coat length. It is a practical gift for anyone with fur on the couch.

Pick 4

Greenies Large Original Dental Dog Treats, 24 Count

Under $50

Greenies is a familiar dental chew brand with a high Amazon rating. The 24-count large pack lasts a while, which makes it an easy gift for a dog owner.

Watch out: Large treats are sized by dog weight. Check the size guide on the listing, and give treats only as the maker directs.

If you buy one, the KONG is the safest toy. For an owner with a shedding dog, the FURminator.
