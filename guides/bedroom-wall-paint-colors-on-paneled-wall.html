8 Bedroom Wall Colors on One Paneled Wall, Navy to Alabaster

A paint chip is hard to judge on a real wall. This guide shows one paneled bedroom wall in eight Sherwin-Williams colors, so you can compare how each one reads next to the same bed, wood and brass.

The images are AI-generated concept pictures, not photos of a real room. The code, hex value and light reflectance for each color come from Sherwin-Williams' own color pages, read Oct 25, 2026. Screens change color, so always test a sample on your own wall before you buy a gallon.

Eight wall colors, one room

Light reflectance (LRV) runs from 0 to 100. Lower numbers mean a darker color. The ceiling follows the room: warm white on the light colors, the same color as the walls on the three dark ones.

If you buy one

If you buy one, make it the sconces. Lighting changes how every color in the gallery reads at night, and it works on any wall color. Add the grasscloth for an accent wall if you rent, and the strips for art once the wall is ready.

What I skipped

  • Paint and primer sold by the gallon, since color depends on your own sample test.
  • Any wall product that does not say it is removable or peel and stick.
  • Anything with no size or hanging details on the listing.
