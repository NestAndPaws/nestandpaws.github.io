Front Door Paint Colors to Compare: 8 Sherwin-Williams Picks

Same door, same light, eight colors. Compare them on screen, then sample the two you like on the real door.

By Nest & Paws · Checked Nov 14, 2026 · How I pick

A paint chip is small, and a front door is the first thing people see. This guide shows one white house with one six-panel door painted in eight Sherwin-Williams colors, so you can compare how each one reads against the same siding, trim and brass.

The images are AI-generated concept pictures, not photos of a real house. The code, hex value and light reflectance for each color come from Sherwin-Williams' own color pages, read Nov 14, 2026. A screen only shows a closest match, never the paint itself, so test a sample on your own door before you buy a can.

Eight door colors, one house

Light reflectance (LRV) runs from 0 to 100, and lower numbers mean a darker color. Seven of the eight are dark, at 12 or lower. Coral Clay, at 26, is the lightest and the boldest next to white siding.

Before you paint the door

Sample on the door, not on a chip

Paint a patch of the door itself and look at it in the morning, at midday and at dusk. Check it again at night under your porch light. A color can look different in each.

Check the siding and trim

The concept images use white siding and white trim. Brick, stone, gray or tan siding changes how each color reads, so hold the sample next to your own house.

Buy paint made for exteriors

Look for an exterior label on the can, and check the color page for whether the color comes in an exterior line. Read the can for the sheen, the temperature range and the dry times.

Prep the surface

Clean the door, lightly sand any glossy finish, and spot-prime bare wood or metal before you paint. Follow the can for how long to wait between coats.

Plan for the hardware

Brass handles stand out against a dark door. Take the handleset off, or tape it well, so the paint does not end up on the metal.

Check your door maker and your rules

Some door materials have limits on dark colors, so check the maker's paint guidance. If you rent or live in an HOA, ask before you paint.

If you do one thing

If you do one thing, sample your top two colors on the door itself and look at them at three times of day. A color that wins on a screen can lose in your own light.

What I left out

  • Paint, primer and brushes sold by the can, since the right product depends on your door material and the maker's directions.
  • Any color without a code and a reading on the maker's own page.
  • Product links, since this guide is about the color choice.

How I chose the colors

  • The eight colors come from Sherwin-Williams' own front door suggestions and its popular door colors, chosen to cover black, blue, green, red, warm and brown.
  • Codes, hex values and light reflectance were read from the Sherwin-Williams color pages on Nov 14, 2026.
  • The door is recolored on one image in code, so the siding, trim, light and brass stay the same in every look.
