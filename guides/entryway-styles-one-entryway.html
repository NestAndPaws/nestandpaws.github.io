If you buy one

If you buy one, make it the hooks. A row of hooks does the most to make a small entry work every day, and it comes off a rental wall. Add the key bowl to keep the small things in one place.

What I skipped

  • Consoles, benches, mirrors and rugs, since the images show style ideas, not products.
  • Anything that needs drilling or wiring.
  • A mirror cabinet that hangs over a door, which does not fit an open entry wall.
