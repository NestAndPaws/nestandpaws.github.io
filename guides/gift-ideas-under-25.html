Pick 1

Lodge 8-Inch Pre-Seasoned Cast Iron Skillet

Under $25

Lodge's 8-inch skillet is a small, simple pan for one or two servings. The listing says it comes pre-seasoned, so it is ready to cook in. It is a gift idea for someone setting up a first kitchen.

Watch out: Cast iron needs hand washing and a light coat of oil after use. Check the care notes on the listing.

KONG Classic Dog Toy, Large

Chuckit! Ultra Ball, Medium, 2-Pack

If you buy one

If you buy one, the Lodge skillet is the safest gift: it has a long-running brand, a small size and the same price at two retailers. For a dog owner, start with the KONG.

What I skipped

  • Items sold only by marketplace sellers with few reviews.
  • Items sold at only one major retailer.
  • Items whose price sits outside the band at either retailer on Nov 1, 2026.

How I picked

  • Sold at Amazon and at least one other major retailer, or at two non-Amazon retailers.
  • A price inside the band at both retailers on Nov 1, 2026.
  • A listing rating of 4.2 stars or higher at each retailer that shows one.
