Bathroom Vanity Paint Colors: 8 Sherwin-Williams Picks

Same tile, mirror, floor and hardware. Only the vanity color changes. Compare eight on screen, then sample the two you like on the real door.

By Nest & Paws · Checked Nov 18, 2026 · How I pick

A paint chip is small, and a vanity is the one piece of color in a plain bathroom. This guide shows one 30 inch shaker vanity with the same white tile, mirror, countertop and floor in eight Sherwin-Williams colors, so the vanity is the only thing that changes.

The images are AI-generated concept pictures, not photos of a real bathroom. The code and hex value for each color come from Sherwin-Williams' own color pages, read Oct 25, 2026. A screen only shows a closest match, never the paint itself, so test a sample before you buy a can.

Eight vanity colors, one bathroom

The eight run from Alabaster, a warm white, to dark colors like Naval and Hunt Club. The tile, mirror, floor and countertop stay the same in every look.

Before you paint the vanity

Sample on the door, not on a chip

Paint a small patch on the back of a door or on a spare board and look at it under your own vanity lights and in daylight. Bathroom light is often warm or cool in a way that shifts the color.

Check the hardware and the tile

The concept images use white tile and brushed metal pulls. If your tile, countertop or pulls lean warm or cool, hold the sample next to them, since the same color can look different beside each.

Prep matters more than color

Clean off soap and oil, scuff the old finish, and check the can or the maker's page for the primer it suggests for cabinets. Peeling is more often a prep problem than a color problem.

Pick a finish made for wet rooms

Bathrooms see steam and splashes. Check the can for the finish and whether it is made for cabinets or trim, and ask the store what the maker suggests for a bathroom.

Go dark only if you have light

Dark colors like Naval and Hunt Club look rich with good light and heavy in a small windowless bath. They also show water spots and dust more.

Mind the dry and cure time

Doors and drawers need to dry and cure before heavy use. Check the can for the recoat time and the time before the paint is fully hard, and plan a few days without using the vanity.

If you do one thing

If you do one thing, sample your top two colors on a cabinet door and look at them at three times of day under your own bulbs. A color that wins on a screen can lose in your own light.

What I left out

  • Paint, primer and brushes sold by the can, since the right product depends on your cabinet and the maker's directions.
  • Any color without a code and a reading on the maker's own page.
  • Product links, since this guide is about the color choice.

How I chose the colors

  • The eight colors cover a warm white, a soft greige, two pale greens, a terracotta, a dark blue, a deep green and a dark bronze gray, so you can compare very different directions on one vanity.
  • Codes and hex values were read from the Sherwin-Williams color pages on Oct 25, 2026. Light reflectance is listed only where it was read.
  • The vanity is recolored on one image in code, so the tile, mirror, countertop and floor stay the same in every look.
