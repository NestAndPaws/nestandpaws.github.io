If you buy one

If you buy one, make it the lamps. Lighting changes how every style reads at night, and cordless lamps work on any side table. Add the curtain rod for a Coastal or Japandi look and the sconces for a Classic one.

What I skipped

  • Sofas and large furniture, since the images are style ideas, not products.
  • Anything that needs drilling or wiring.
  • Anything with no size or hanging details on the listing.
