Pick 1

Anker Nano Smart Display Charger, 45W

Under $50

Anker's 45W Nano charger has a small display and a foldable plug. The Amazon listing shows a 4.7 star rating from more than 6,000 ratings on Nov 11, 2026. It is a gift idea for someone who wants one slim charger for a phone and a laptop.

Watch out: The listing says smart recognition works with supported iPhones only. Best Buy sold the blue version on Nov 11, so check the color on the listing.

Mophie 3-in-1 Travel Charger with MagSafe

Watch out: Some lower-rated Amazon reviews say it did not charge an Apple Watch reliably. The listing rating is 4.0 stars from 246 ratings on Nov 11, 2026.

Quntis Monitor Light Bar, 15.7 Inch

Under $50

Quntis sells a 15.7 inch light bar that clips on top of a monitor. The listing names a dual front light, a color backlight with 24 RGB modes and up to 900 lux. The Amazon listing shows 4.5 stars from more than 1,200 ratings on Nov 11, 2026.

Watch out: Several lower-rated reviews say the touch controls respond inconsistently. Check the control notes on the listing.

Pick 4

BenQ ScreenBar Halo 2

Under $250

BenQ's Halo 2 is a monitor light bar with a wireless controller puck and two light sources. The Best Buy page says it fits monitors 0.17 to 2.36 inches thick, flat or curved. The Amazon listing shows 4.7 stars from more than 1,300 ratings on Nov 11, 2026.

Watch out: Lower-rated reviews call it bulky, and one reviewer saw light reach their eyes even after aiming it away.

Pick 5

Lofree Flow Lite 100 Keyboard

Under $150

Lofree's Flow Lite 100 is a low-profile keyboard with 100 keys, a gasket mount, a volume roller and three connection modes. The Amazon listing shows 4.4 stars from more than 700 ratings on Nov 11, 2026.

Watch out: Lower-rated reviews mention tight key spacing that causes typos, and one reports a slow wake-up from sleep.

If you buy one

If you buy one, start with the Anker Nano charger: it is the smallest change and ties for the highest rating of the five. For a desk that looks finished, add the BenQ light.

What I skipped

  • Items sold only by marketplace sellers with few reviews.
  • Picks whose listing could not be matched across two retailers (the Quntis light bar is Amazon only for that reason).
  • Desk gadgets with no clear use at a desk, such as novelty USB items.

How I picked

  • Sold at Amazon and at least one other retailer when a match could be verified.
  • A listing rating of 4.0 stars or higher at Amazon on Nov 11, 2026.
  • A recurring mention in desk setup roundups and a rising Pinterest search term for cozy and office desk setups.
