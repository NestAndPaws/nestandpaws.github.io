If you buy one

If you only buy one, make it the straight jean. It works with every look in the gallery. Add the cable knit next, then boots and a scarf once the weather turns.

What I skipped

  • Anything where the listing does not say what the item is made of or how it is sized.
  • Brand-name designer pieces, since this guide is about fall basics at everyday prices.
  • Anything sold only in one color or size range with no size chart on the listing.
