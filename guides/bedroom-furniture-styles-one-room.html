If you buy one

If you buy one, make it the lamps. Lighting changes how every style reads at night, and cordless lamps work on any nightstand. Add the sconces for a Classic look and the shelf for a Modern one.

What I skipped

  • Beds and large furniture, since the images are style ideas, not products.
  • Anything that needs drilling or wiring.
  • Anything with no size or hanging details on the listing.
