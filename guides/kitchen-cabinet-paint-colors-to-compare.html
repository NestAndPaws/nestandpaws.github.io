8 Kitchen Cabinet Paint Colors on One Kitchen, Navy to Alabaster

Same kitchen, same light, eight cabinet colors. Compare them first, then pick the small upgrades that fit.

A paint chip is hard to judge on a full run of cabinets. This guide shows one kitchen with shaker cabinets in eight Sherwin-Williams colors, so you can compare how each one reads next to the same tile, butcher block and brass.

The images are AI-generated concept pictures, not photos of a real kitchen. The code, hex value and light reflectance for each color come from Sherwin-Williams' own color pages, read Oct 25 and Nov 12, 2026. Screens change color, and the closest match on a screen is never the paint itself, so test a sample on your own cabinet door before you buy a gallon.

Eight cabinet colors, one kitchen

Light reflectance (LRV) runs from 0 to 100. Lower numbers mean a darker color. Five of the eight are dark on purpose, since dark cabinets are the look most people ask about. Dark colors tend to show dust and fingerprints. Light colors tend to show grease near the stove.

If you buy one

If you buy one, make it the under-cabinet lights. Lighting changes how every color in the gallery reads at night, and it works with any cabinet color. Add the pulls to test a new look once the paint has cured, and the organizer for the day the doors come off.

What I skipped

  • Paint, primer and cleaner sold by the can, since color and prep depend on your own sample test and the maker's directions.
  • Any cabinet product that does not list its size or how it mounts.
  • Anything with no limit or care note on the listing.
