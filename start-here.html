What Nest & Paws is

A small, independent guide for renters and small homes.

Each guide starts with a problem, like hooks that hold without holes or a drafty door. It shows three to five picks that fit, with the weight limit, the catch, and how the item comes off.

How I pick

  • 4.3 stars or higher and at least 3,000 ratings on the day I check.
  • The listing states a weight limit and the surfaces it works on.
  • The maker or the listing says how it comes off.
  • I read Amazon's review summary to find the most common failure, and I put it in the guide.
  • Prices appear as brackets, like "Under $10," because Amazon prices change.

I read, I do not test

I have not used these products. Every guide says so. If I buy and test something, the guide will say that too, and say what I did.

The deposit-safe score

A 1 to 5 score for how likely an item is to leave no mark if you install and remove it the way the maker says. A 5 touches nothing, like an over-door rack. A 3 uses adhesive and the listing gives no removal method. It is based on the mounting method and the maker's guidance, not on tests.

About the images

The pictures on this site are AI-assisted illustrations that show how a type of product might look in a home. They are not photos of a specific unit. Check the photos, size, and details on the Amazon listing before you buy.

Links and money

Links go straight to Amazon. Right now they do not earn this site anything. If that changes, a plain disclosure will appear above any link that does. No brand pays for placement. Nest & Paws is not affiliated with or endorsed by Amazon.
