About Nest & Paws

Last updated October 13, 2026

Nest & Paws is a small, independent guide for renters and small homes. It collects Amazon finds for home and kitchen, pets, beauty, and renter-friendly living, and each pick comes with a short note on what it fixes and who it suits.

How picks are chosen

  • Picks are added a few at a time, not pulled from a bestseller feed. No brand pays for placement.
  • Every product has to be on Amazon when it is added and solve one specific, nameable problem.
  • Renter-friendly picks must be removable, no-drill, or peel-and-stick, so they work in apartments, dorms, and rentals.
  • I read listings and the maker's instructions. I do not test the products. See Start here for the full method.
  • Prices appear as brackets (for example "Under $25") because Amazon prices change. Check the current price on Amazon.

Where to start

  • Guides: short buying guides, one problem each
  • Renter Friendly: no-drill and removable upgrades
  • Home & Kitchen: storage, cleaning, kitchen tools
  • Pets, Beauty, Under $25, Seasonal, Wedding Table Decor

About the images

The lifestyle pictures on this site are AI-assisted illustrations made to show how a product might look in a home. They are not photos of a specific unit. Always check the product photos, size, and details on the Amazon listing before you buy.

How the site is funded

It is not funded by ads or paid placement. Links go straight to Amazon and do not earn this site anything right now. If that changes, a plain disclosure will appear above any link that earns a commission. Nest & Paws is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Amazon.

Find us

New picks are shared on Pinterest at @NestandPaws. See also the privacy page.
