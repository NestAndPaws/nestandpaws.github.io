About Nest & Paws

Last updated October 4, 2026

Nest & Paws is a small, independent home and living guide. It collects Amazon finds for home and kitchen, pets, beauty and renter-friendly living, and each pick comes with a short note on what it fixes and who it suits.

How picks are chosen

  • Picks are added a few at a time, not pulled from a bestseller feed. No brand pays for placement.
  • Every product has to be available on Amazon when it is added and solve one specific, nameable problem.
  • Renter-friendly picks must be removable, no-drill or peel-and-stick, so they work in apartments, dorms and rentals.
  • Small-space and small-budget usability counts for more than brand name.
  • Picks are grouped into price brackets (for example "Under $25") instead of exact prices, because Amazon prices change often. Check the current price on Amazon before you buy.

Where to start

  • Home & Kitchen: storage, cleaning, kitchen tools and small upgrades
  • Renter Friendly: no-drill and removable upgrades for rentals
  • Pets: dog and cat products for daily use
  • Beauty: hair and skincare, weighted toward hair
  • Under $25: every pick under $25
  • Seasonal and Wedding Table Decor

About the images

The lifestyle photos on this site are AI-assisted illustrations made to show how a product might look in a home. They are not photos of a specific unit. Always check the product photos, size and details on the Amazon listing before you buy.

How the site is funded

Product links on Nest & Paws are affiliate links. If you buy through them, the site earns a small commission at no extra cost to you. That does not change what gets picked or what is written about it. Nest & Paws is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by Amazon.
